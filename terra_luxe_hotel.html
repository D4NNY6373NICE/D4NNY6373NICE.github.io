About

Rooted in place, refined by time

Terra Luxe was founded on a simple idea: that the most memorable stays are the ones that feel inseparable from where they are. We build with local stone, cook from local soil and listen to local stories.

Every property is small enough to know you by name and considered enough to anticipate what you have not yet asked for. Our concierge team is available around the clock, wherever in the world you happen to be.
