Fallen in? Float to Live.

Cold water can make you gasp and panic. Fight the urge to thrash around and give your body time to recover.

More safety advice
In an emergency at sea or on the coast, dial 999 or 112 and ask for the Coastguard.
  1. Tilt your head back with your ears in the water.
  2. Relax and try to breathe normally.
  3. Move your hands gently to help you stay afloat.
  4. Spread your arms and legs out if it helps you balance.
  5. Once your breathing is under control, call for help or swim to safety.
